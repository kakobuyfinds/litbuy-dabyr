03 / A useful browsing method

Turn Litbuy
spreadsheet finds
into informed choices.

A long list is only a starting point. A useful shortlist explains what an item is, how it fits and which details still need checking.

Keep your Litbuy product spreadsheet consistent: use the same fields for every candidate and note when you last checked each listing.

  1. Choose a category before a product.

    Start with shoes, layers or accessories. A focused category makes comparisons easier than an unfiltered feed.

  2. Capture the exact listing.

    Save the item URL, selected variation, stated materials and seller information. Similar photos can describe different products.

  3. Compare measurements and total cost.

    Record the size chart, item price and any known shipping charges. Keep unknown fees marked as unknown.

  4. Verify before you commit.

    Recheck availability, platform support, delivery conditions and returns using current information from the relevant provider.

04 / Compare what matters

A better shopping sheet.

Use this Litbuy shopping guide as a framework for your own records. These are suggested fields, not verified product data.

Suggested fields for a product comparison spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Product identity Item name, exact URL, seller and selected color. Helps you return to the same item and variation.
Fit & construction Size measurements, fabric composition and construction details. Makes comparisons more useful than size labels alone.
Costs Current item price, currency, shipping estimate and known fees. Keeps an item price separate from the estimated total.
Evidence Listing photos, available reviews and unanswered questions. Shows what is supported and what still needs verification.
Order conditions Availability, delivery terms, return conditions and date checked. Flags information that can change before an order.

06 / Quick answers

A few things
to know first.

Clear answers for readers researching a Litbuy spreadsheet, product links and the next steps in their shopping research.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Litbuy spreadsheet generally means a product discovery list organized into rows, often with item links, categories and comparison notes. The contents and accuracy depend on whoever maintains that list.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim affiliation with Litbuy or the fashion brands mentioned.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a browsing guide and comparison checklist, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet. You can create your own sheet using the suggested fields and open CAFANS to research products.

What should I check before selecting an item?

Check the exact listing, size measurements, materials, available options, seller information, current price, shipping terms and return conditions. Do not treat an illustration or discovery list as proof of authenticity or availability.

Does a listed product work with Litbuy?

Compatibility is not verified by this page. Confirm the current platform rules and whether the exact seller link is supported before placing an order.
